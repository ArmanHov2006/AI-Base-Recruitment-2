# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Recruiters and HR managers at a bank. They operate in a high-stakes environment where hiring decisions must be defensible, efficient, and data-driven.

## Product Purpose
The pipeline Kanban is the primary triage surface for managing candidate applications. It allows recruiters to move candidates through stages (Screening, Interview, Offer, etc.) while maintaining a clear view of candidate quality via AI-driven scores.

## Brand Personality
- **Professional & Sharp**: Clean, efficient, and reliable.
- **Modern & AI-forward**: Cutting-edge technology (AI scoring) presented with a sophisticated, data-first aesthetic.
- **Defensible**: Information is presented clearly to support objective decision-making.

## Anti-references
- **Blind Triage**: Cards without ratings or clear identifiers.
- **Cramped Layouts**: Narrow columns that hide information on smaller screens.
- **Ambiguous Identity**: Visually identical cards for different candidates.

## Design Principles
1. **Defensible Triage**: Every card must provide the necessary data (score, unique ID) to justify its position in the pipeline.
2. **Pre-attentive Scanning**: Use color, hierarchy, and typography to make critical information (AI scores) scannable across the entire board.
3. **Adaptive Spatial Clarity**: The board must maintain readability and functionality across all viewports (from mobile to large monitors), using appropriate patterns (e.g., horizontal scrolling, stackable columns).
4. **Unambiguous Identity**: Differentiate every candidate through unique anchors (initials, email, short refs) to prevent collisions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast for critical data (scores).
- Clear focus states for keyboard navigation and drag-and-drop.
- Support for reduced motion (optional but preferred).
